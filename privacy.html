Gold Studio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Contact: berramouhcine13@gmail.com

Data we collect

  • Account data: email address, optional phone number, and a hashed password.
  • TikTok username you enter, and — only after you log in with TikTok and give permission — your public statistics (followers, following, likes, video count) through TikTok's official API (scopes user.info.basic and user.info.stats).
  • In-app activity: points, completed tasks, community posts.

How we use it

To show your account statistics, analyze them, give you growth tips and content suggestions, run points, contests and leaderboards, and process subscriptions.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share your TikTok data with third parties. Your email is never shown to other users. Payments are handled by PayPal.

Your rights

You can delete your account in the app at any time, and revoke Gold Studio's access from your TikTok settings. You may also request deletion by emailing us.
