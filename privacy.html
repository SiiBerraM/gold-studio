1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Gold Studio ("we", "us", "our"), a Utilities web application available at https://siiberram.github.io/gold-studio (the "Service"), collects, uses, stores and protects information, including information obtained through the TikTok APIs when you choose to connect your TikTok account. By using the Service you agree to this Policy.

2. Information We Collect

a) Information you provide: links, usernames or content identifiers you enter into the Service for analysis, and any message you send us.

b) Information from TikTok (only if you connect your account): when you authorize Gold Studio through TikTok Login Kit / TikTok APIs, we receive only the data covered by the permissions (scopes) you approve on TikTok's consent screen. Depending on the features you use, this may include: your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar; public profile information; basic account statistics (such as follower and like counts); and metadata and public statistics of your own videos (such as title, cover image, view, like, comment and share counts). We also receive an access token and refresh token that allow us to call the TikTok APIs on your behalf.

c) Technical data: browser type, device type, language, and basic logs needed to operate and secure the Service. We may store preferences locally in your browser (local storage).

We do not collect your TikTok password, and we do not access your private messages or any data outside the scopes you approve.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide the analysis features of the Service and show you results about your own account or the content you submit.
  • To authenticate you and keep your TikTok connection working (using access/refresh tokens).
  • To maintain, secure, debug and improve the Service.
  • To respond to your requests and comply with legal obligations.

We use TikTok data only for the purposes you authorized and described above. We do not use it for advertising, profiling unrelated to the Service, or any purpose that violates TikTok's terms or policies.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not sell TikTok user data. We may share limited information only with: (i) infrastructure and hosting providers that process data on our behalf to run the Service (for example GitHub Pages or similar hosting); (ii) authorities when required by law; and (iii) a successor entity in a merger or acquisition, subject to this Policy. We do not transfer TikTok data to data brokers or use it to build databases for resale.

5. Data Retention

We keep TikTok-derived data and tokens only as long as needed to provide the Service or until you disconnect your account or ask us to delete it. Tokens are deleted when you disconnect or when they expire or are revoked. Technical logs are kept for a limited period needed for security and debugging.

6. Disconnecting and Deleting Your Data

  • Disconnect: you can revoke Gold Studio's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services (wording may vary by app version), or through the disconnect option in the Service if available.
  • Delete: email berramouhcine13@gmail.com from your contact address with the subject "Data deletion request". We will delete the data we hold about you within a reasonable time, normally within 30 days.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures (such as HTTPS and restricted access to tokens) to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required by TikTok or applicable law in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including where our hosting providers operate. We take reasonable steps to ensure it is handled in line with this Policy.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.
