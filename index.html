What Gold Studio does

Account dashboard

Followers, following, likes, videos, views and comments in one place, with a clear overall score and your progress between visits.

Video performance analysis

We compare your recent videos with each other: best and weakest videos, median views, engagement, posting consistency and momentum.

Practical recommendations

Each recommendation comes with the reason, one concrete step to take, and how to measure whether it worked.

Tasks, points and levels

Complete tasks to earn points, climb levels and join community contests.

How it works

  1. Open the app at app.html and create an account, or continue with TikTok.
  2. Log in with TikTok using TikTok's official Login Kit. You sign in on TikTok itself, and you see exactly which permissions Gold Studio asks for.
  3. Approve access. Gold Studio reads your profile name, your account statistics and the statistics of your own videos.
  4. Get your results. Your dashboard and recommendations appear in the app and update whenever you refresh them.

Your data and permissions

Gold Studio only asks for read access. It never posts, comments, follows or changes anything on your TikTok account, and it never asks for your TikTok password.

PermissionWhat we readWhy
user.info.basicDisplay name and avatarShows which TikTok account is linked.
user.info.statsFollower, following, likes and video countsShown on your dashboard.
video.listViews, likes, comments and shares of your own videosUsed to compute median views, engagement and posting consistency, and to write your recommendations.

Your data is used only inside the app for you, and is never sold. You can remove Gold Studio's access at any time from your TikTok account settings. Read the full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

What we do not do: we do not sell or promise followers, likes, views or comments, and we do not guarantee growth results.

Frequently asked questions

Is Gold Studio affiliated with TikTok?

No. Gold Studio is an independent service that uses TikTok's official developer APIs with your permission.


Can I delete my account?

Yes. You can delete your Gold Studio account from the app at any time, and revoke access from your TikTok settings.


Why are some numbers missing?

TikTok does not share watch time, completion rate or saves with third-party apps. Check those inside TikTok's own analytics.
